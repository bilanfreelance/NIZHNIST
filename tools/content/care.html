Caring for linen.

Linen gets better with time. A few gentle habits keep it soft, strong and beautiful.

Shipping & ReturnsSize GuideCare GuideFAQContact
01Wash cool

30°C on a delicate cycle with a mild detergent. Wash similar colours together.

02Dry naturally

Reshape while damp and hang to dry. Avoid tumble drying to keep the fit.

03Steam, don’t press

A quick steam relaxes creases. If you iron, do so while slightly damp.

04Store loosely

Fold or hang in a breathable space — linen likes air.

General linen care advice — always follow the label on your garment.
