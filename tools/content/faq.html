Orders

Can I change or cancel my order?

Write to us within 2 hours of ordering and we’ll do our best to update it before it ships.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal (demo store).

Sizing

How do your pieces fit?

Our silhouettes are relaxed. Take your usual size for an easy drape, or size down for a closer fit. Each product page has fit notes.

Are sets sold in one size?

Yes — sets are sold in one combined size for both pieces. If you need different sizes, contact us.

Linen

Will linen shrink?

Our linen is pre-washed. Wash cool and dry naturally to keep the fit.

Why does linen crease?

It’s part of its character. Creases soften with wear; a little steam smooths them out.

Shipping & returns

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to most of Europe, the UK and the US. Delivery times are shown at checkout.

How do returns work?

You have 30 days to return unworn pieces in their original condition. See Shipping & Returns.
