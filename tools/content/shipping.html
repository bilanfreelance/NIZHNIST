Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 (Kyiv time) on business days ship the same day. You’ll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the studio.

Returns & exchanges

Changed your mind? Return unworn pieces with their tags within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from your account or write to hello@nizhnist.com; refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving your parcel.

Exchanges

Need a different size? We’ll reserve it for you while your return is on its way.
