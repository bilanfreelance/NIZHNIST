Our story

Made to Feel Free.

NIZHNIST — ніжність — means tenderness. We make linen clothing for women who want to feel at ease in what they wear: natural fabric, considered silhouettes and a little more room to breathe.

01

Natural first

We work with linen because it breathes, keeps you cool and softens with every wash. Its texture is part of the design, not something to hide.

02

Fewer, better pieces

A small collection of sets, dresses and shirts that work together — designed to be worn often, layered easily and kept for years.

03

Designed in Ukraine

Every silhouette starts in our studio. We pattern, fit and refine each piece until it moves the way linen should.

The process

From flax to finish.

  1. FabricWashed linen chosen for its hand-feel and natural irregularity.
  2. PatternRelaxed, generous cuts — tested on real bodies, not just mannequins.
  3. FinishSoft gathers, deep pockets and buttons that are easy to live with.
